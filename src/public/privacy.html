Your data

This site sets no cookies. Fonts, images and scripts come from the site itself. Your browser keeps the language you chose, the quizzes you opened for others to join, with what it takes to come back to them, how you set up the screens, and on which of the last 60 days you uploaded a quiz; for each tab, the part it plays in a quiz, so that a reload brings it back. Of this, the site gets only what a quiz needs, such as the way back into it. A quiz you open on the start page passes to the next page through your browser's storage, which deletes it as that page takes it; should that page never open, it goes the next time the start page opens.

Played on your own, a quiz never leaves your browser.

Played with others, a quiz, with its pictures and the players' avatars, answers and scores, is stored on the site and deleted when it ends, at the latest three hours after its last use. Before a picture leaves your browser, the browser draws it anew, which drops what a photo carries besides the picture, such as camera, time and place.

No request is logged with an IP address. To limit guesses at the code that steers a quiz from another device, the site tells devices apart by a hash of their address, salted for that quiz, and keeps it while the device is connected or its wrong guesses count, at the latest until the quiz is deleted.

To see how the site is used and where it fails, it counts by the day: how many quizzes were uploaded and played and in which way, with how many players and devices, how far they got, and how their connections held. Nothing in these counts ties them to a person, a device or a quiz, and none is finer than the day. A day's counts are kept 31 days, then added to their month's; a month's are kept 24 months. Only I see them, except for what anyone can see: how full the site is, today and on recent days, and the number of upload attempts per file format, with the first and last day each format was seen: /api/stats.

The site runs on Cloudflare, Inc. (USA). Every request passes through its network, and it keeps the site's data in data centres in the EU. How Cloudflare itself handles requests is set out in its privacy policy.

If you write to me, I use what you tell me to answer you. I don't pass your details on.
