A question on screen

How the classyQuiz projector lays out a question, with or without its picture, and how a student's device shows its choices: the markup, and the rules from the site's stylesheet. What the stylesheet doesn't carry is in comments: the type sizes and the picture's size and place, which a script sets from the length of the text and the picture's shape, and what the reveal adds.

Markup

<!-- The projector, one question. Always the light theme: projectors wash out dark backgrounds. -->
<body class="screen"><main>
  <div class="bar" id="qbar"><span id="qpos">2 / 12</span><span class="strip" id="strip"><!-- an avatar per answer that has arrived --></span><span id="qicons"></span><span class="qcount">👤 <span>17 / 24</span></span></div>
  <div class="stage">
    <h2 class="qtext">A jacket costs 80 francs and is reduced by 25%. What does it cost now?</h2>
    <div class="tiles">
      <div class="tile a0"><svg class="shape" viewBox="0 0 100 100"><path d="M50 12 L90 84 H10Z"/></svg><span class="txt">55 francs</span><span class="side"></span></div>
      <div class="tile a1"><svg class="shape" viewBox="0 0 100 100"><path d="M50 8 L92 50 L50 92 L8 50Z"/></svg><span class="txt">20 francs</span><span class="side"></span></div>
      <div class="tile a2"><svg class="shape" viewBox="0 0 100 100"><circle cx="50" cy="50" r="40"/></svg><span class="txt">75 francs</span><span class="side"></span></div>
      <div class="tile a3"><svg class="shape" viewBox="0 0 100 100"><rect x="13" y="13" width="74" height="74" rx="4"/></svg><span class="txt">60 francs</span><span class="side"></span></div>
    </div>
  </div>
</main></body>
<!-- The bar has a fixed height (1.9em). While the class answers, the avatar of each student whose answer has
     arrived pops into .strip, sized so the whole class fits, in one row or more.
     When answering closes: every tile's .side becomes <span class="n">17</span> (how many picked it), and each tile gets
     <span class="fill" style="width: 100 × its count ÷ the highest count %"></span>, growing from 0.
     At the solution: a tile that doesn't count gets the class "dim", and a tile that counts gets
     <span class="mark">✓</span> before its count. The tiles are marked in place: nothing else is redrawn, so
     neither text nor picture moves. -->

<!-- Every question first comes up with its choices hidden, while the teacher reads it out: the same layout, the
     tiles with the class "veiled" (their text hidden, colour and shape faded), laid out for their choices so
     nothing moves when the teacher shows them. During Countdown a bar drains along the top edge of the screen
     for fifteen seconds, over everything and outside the layout:
     <div id="clock"><div class="clockbar wall"><span></span></div></div>. -->

<!-- A question with a picture (its Image: line). Between the question and the tiles: -->
  <div class="stage fig between">
    <h2 class="qtext">Welche Zahl ist mit dem Pfeil markiert?</h2>
    <div class="figure" style="width: 1298px; height: 214px"><img src="…" width="2000" height="330" alt=""></div>
    <div class="tiles"> … the tiles as above … </div>
  </div>
<!-- Or beside them, the picture on the left: -->
  <div class="stage fig beside" style="grid-template-columns: auto minmax(0, 1fr)">
    <div class="figure" style="width: 606px; height: 606px"><img …></div>
    <div class="col"><h2 class="qtext">…</h2><div class="tiles"> … one column … </div></div>
  </div>
<!-- Which one, and the figure's size in px, the script works out from the picture's shape and the room the
     question leaves (sizes for a 1366×768 screen above). It lays out "between": the question first, at most
     30% of the height; the tiles keep at least 13.5% of the height per row for choices up to 12 characters,
     16% up to 30, 20% beyond; the picture gets the rest, at most 62% of the height, never wider than the screen.
     It then takes "beside" if that shows the picture at least 1.3 times larger, with the picture at 58% (else 50%)
     of the width, and only if the question stays at 1.5em or more and no choice drops below 1.3em in the
     narrower column. So wide figures such as number lines go between, square and upright ones and photos
     beside, and a long question keeps its picture between. A picture smaller than its box is enlarged.

     Z, or a click on the picture, enlarges it over the whole screen until the next step:
       <div id="zoom"><div class="figure" style="(as large as fits in 96% × 94% of the screen)"><img …></div></div>

     With a background (a Background: line), the body gets the class "has-backdrop" and, first in it:
       <div id="backdrop"><img …></div>
     The question and the bar then sit on white cards. -->

<!-- Pictures as choices ("+ Image: pie-3.png"): each picture sits on its tile's colour, which shows wherever
     the picture is transparent; a picture with a ground of its own (white or any other) shows as drawn. The shape
     and the room for the answers' count and tick sit beside the picture ("row") or above it ("col"). A text
     choice among them ("none of these") takes a picture's place. -->
  <div class="stage picans">
    <h2 class="qtext">Which picture shows three quarters?</h2>
    <div class="tiles pics" style="grid-template-columns: repeat(4, minmax(0, 1fr)); grid-template-rows: repeat(1, minmax(0, 1fr)); height: (the room below the question, in px)">
      <div class="tile pic a0 col"><svg class="shape" …></svg><div class="apic"><img src="…" alt=""></div><span class="side"></span></div>
      … one tile per choice; for a text choice <div class="apic text"><span class="txt">None of these</span></div> …
    </div>
  </div>
<!-- The script arranges the tiles by the pictures' shape: one, two or four columns (three for three choices),
     each tile "row" or "col", whichever shows the smallest picture largest; two by two when that is within 85%
     of the best. Square pictures come out two by two or four in a row, wide strips two by two or one under the
     other, upright ones side by side. With the question's own picture (an Image: line), that picture goes into
     a band beside the question's text, <div class="qband"><div class="figure">…</div><h2 class="qtext">…</h2></div>,
     or between text and tiles at the full width, the room split so that it and the smallest picture choice come
     out about alike. The class's answers and the solution fill .side and add .fill and .dim as for text tiles. -->

<!-- A student's phone or tablet: the choices as buttons, without the question's picture; the question is on
     the projector. Picture choices show their pictures, in a smaller copy, on the buttons:
     <button class="ansbtn pic a0 col"><svg class="shape" …></svg><span class="apic"><img …></span></button>,
     arranged by the pictures' shape like the projector's tiles, though not always the same way. -->
<body class="play"><main>
  <div class="buttons n4">
    <button class="ansbtn a0"><svg class="shape" viewBox="0 0 100 100"><path d="M50 12 L90 84 H10Z"/></svg><span>55 francs</span></button>
    <button class="ansbtn a1"><svg class="shape" viewBox="0 0 100 100"><path d="M50 8 L92 50 L50 92 L8 50Z"/></svg><span>20 francs</span></button>
    <button class="ansbtn a2"><svg class="shape" viewBox="0 0 100 100"><circle cx="50" cy="50" r="40"/></svg><span>75 francs</span></button>
    <button class="ansbtn a3"><svg class="shape" viewBox="0 0 100 100"><rect x="13" y="13" width="74" height="74" rx="4"/></svg><span>60 francs</span></button>
  </div>
</main></body>
<!-- .a0 to .a3 are places on screen, not places in the quiz text: the site shuffles each question's choices at
     upload (and again for another class), the same on the projector, every tablet and the teacher's screens.
     A question with the line "Order: fixed" keeps the text's order. -->

/* .qtext font-size is set by script from the question's length: up to 40 characters 3.6em, up to 90 3em,
   up to 160 2.4em, longer 1.9em; then reduced in 0.15em steps until the question fits in 34vh, down to 1.1em.
   With a picture: 3em, 2.5em, 2.1em, 1.8em, fitting in 30% of the stage's height (42% beside). */
.tiles { flex: 1; display: grid; grid-template-columns: 1fr 1fr; gap: 1.6vh; min-height: 0; }
/* .tiles font-size is set by script from the longest choice: up to 12 characters 2.6em, up to 30 2em,

   if it still overflows, words are broken. A number written in groups ("73 000") stays on one line. */
