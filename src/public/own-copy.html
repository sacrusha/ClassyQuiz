Your own classyQuiz

This site is free and shared by everyone who uses it, with space for a few dozen quizzes a day. Your own copy is the same site at an address of your own, a few dozen quizzes a day just for you. You can set one up yourself, with an AI doing the work, or ask the IT of your school or company to run one.

Setting one up yourself

What you need

  • A computer to deploy through wrangler. On a paid subscription, an AI like Claude Code sets this up on its own virtual machine in minutes. Without one, you can have an AI talk you through setting it up on your own computer, which can take a while.
  • A free Cloudflare account.

What to ask your AI

Please deploy classyQuiz from https://github.com/sacrusha/ClassyQuiz on a free Cloudflare account. Let me know if there's another environment of yours that makes this easier.

What you do yourself

  1. Create a free account at dash.cloudflare.com.
  2. Create an access token: the AI walks you through where and how. It's trickier than you'd think. Don't paste it into the chat; ask the AI how to hand it over securely.
  3. Choose a name for your site. It becomes the address: name.pages.dev.

Or ask your school's or company's IT

People with an IT background and a developer AI can adjust the project to run on their own servers, or run it on a paid Cloudflare account to serve thousands of quizzes per day.

Afterwards

When you ask your AI for a quiz, use the address your copy's start page shows instead of classyquiz.pages.dev.

A copy changes only when its owner updates it. Left as it is, it shows the same screens and buttons every time. Updated, it gets what is new and fixed, and its screens may change. To update a copy you set up yourself, ask your AI to update it from GitHub, the same way you set it up.
