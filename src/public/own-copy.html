Your own classyQuiz

This site is free and shared by everyone who uses it, with room for a few dozen classes a day. When that is used up, it says so and takes new quizzes again once the day turns or older quizzes have expired. Your own copy is the same site at an address of your own, on a free account of your own, and what it allows a day is yours alone. Your school can run one for the school, or you can set one up yourself.

Your school's own copy

Your school's IT can run classyQuiz for the whole school: on the school's own server, or on a free Cloudflare account like this site. Nothing gets installed on your computer. Schools often prefer their own server, since an outside service may first need a data protection review. What to send them:

classyQuiz (github.com/sacrusha/ClassyQuiz) is an open-source site for live classroom quizzes: the quiz runs on the projector and students answer on their own devices, with no accounts, and each quiz is deleted three hours after its last use. Could the school run its own copy? The README says how to set it up on a free Cloudflare account, and how to have an AI convert it to run on the school's own server.

Setting one up yourself

Who does what

  • GitHub is where the site's code is published. Your AI fetches it from there. You don't need a GitHub account.
  • Cloudflare is the company whose free plan runs the site. Your copy runs on your own free Cloudflare account, at an address like yourname.pages.dev.
  • Your AI does the setup: it downloads the code, installs what it needs and puts the site online.

What you need

  • An AI that can run programs on your computer, such as Claude Code. A chat in the browser can explain the steps but can't carry them out.
  • A computer where you may install programs. A school-managed laptop often isn't one.
  • An email address for the Cloudflare account.

What to ask your AI

Please set up my own copy of classyQuiz from https://github.com/sacrusha/ClassyQuiz on a free Cloudflare account, following its README. Tell me each step I have to do myself.

What you do yourself

  1. Create a free account at dash.cloudflare.com, if you don't have one.
  2. Create a token when the AI asks for one: a key that lets it put sites on your account. The AI tells you where in Cloudflare to create it, what to tick, and where to put it.
  3. Choose a name for your site. It becomes the address: name.pages.dev.
  4. The AI asks before it installs programs on your computer (Node.js, git) and runs the setup; those are the steps it needs.

At the end the AI tells you your site's address.

Afterwards

When you ask your AI for a quiz, use the address your copy's start page shows instead of classyquiz.pages.dev.

A copy changes only when its owner updates it. Left as it is, it shows the same screens and buttons every lesson. Updated, it gets what is new and fixed, and its screens may change. To update a copy you set up yourself, ask your AI to update it from GitHub.
